My Background

I am currently in my second year of a Computer Science degree. Before university, I did not know how to write a single line of code. My first programming class changed that — I discovered that I enjoy the process of breaking a problem into small pieces and building a solution step by step.

Since then, I have taken courses in data structures, web development, and object-oriented programming. I still have a lot to learn, and that is exactly what I enjoy about this field: there is always a new concept or tool to understand.
